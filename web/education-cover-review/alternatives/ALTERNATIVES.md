## Caveats

- **"No to-check category" is not proof of review.** It means the file is
  not on the contest's pending list; Commons does not record a positive
  "checked" mark. The only file with no contest link at all is C-A; R-E's
  contest (2022) closed years ago.
- **No Kenyan rabbit photo** without a pending flag was found; the
  recommended rabbit alternatives are from Uganda (alt text must say so).
- Faces/people were checked on the 960 px previews only; the full originals
  get the same check after download.

## Decision (2026-10-12)

Selected **C-A** (chicken) and **R-A** (rabbits). Both originals were
re-checked, downloaded, SHA-1-verified and inspected at full size (no
people, no GPS), prepared, and swapped into `../prepared/`; the previous
chicken and rabbit covers are kept in `../superseded/`.
