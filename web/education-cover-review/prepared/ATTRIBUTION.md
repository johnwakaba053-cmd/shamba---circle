## Licence terms carried

- Credit names the creator and Wikimedia Commons; the guide page links the
  credit to the file page and the licence label to its deed.
- "(cropped, resized)" marks the change, as CC BY 2.0 and CC BY-SA 2.0/4.0
  require.
- ShareAlike: the prepared copies of CC BY-SA originals are adaptations and
  are themselves under the same licence (CC BY-SA 4.0 for chicken, rabbits
  and avocado; CC BY-SA 2.0 for maize). The beekeeping copy stays CC BY 2.0.
- No technical restriction is added (files are signed-URL access only, as
  for all signed-in content).

Processing: full-width 16:9 crop (no rotation beyond the original's own
orientation tag, no retouching, no colour grading), Lanczos downscale
(never upscaled), WebP quality 82 — except the chicken 1280 file, at
quality 45: its grainy, heavily sharpened phone original needed 430 KB at
quality 82, so the replacements were encoded to a 290,000-byte target
(margin under both 300,000 B and the bucket's 307,200 B limit). A 100 %
side-by-side against the uncompressed resize showed no visible difference
in feathers, combs or grass even at quality 40. The rabbit original is tagged sRGB (profile dropped); the chicken original
is untagged. The avocado original is stored sideways
with an orientation tag, so it was turned upright first; its crop box is in
upright (3120×4160) coordinates. Maize's original is tagged Adobe RGB (1998)
and was converted to sRGB so colours stay true without a profile; the others
were sRGB or untagged.

Metadata: written with no EXIF, XMP or ICC data. Checked two ways: Pillow
reports no `exif`/`xmp`/`icc_profile` and 0 EXIF tags, and each file's RIFF
container holds a single `VP8 ` image chunk and nothing else.
