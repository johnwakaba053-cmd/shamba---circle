   (people, faces, quality, whether the subject survives a 16:9 crop) is
   unchecked. Faces of identifiable people → choose another file.
2. **Three files are Wiki Loves Africa 2025 uploads still in the contest's
   "to check" category** (1, 2, 4). The licences are stated by the
   uploaders as their own work; contest review can still remove a file.
   Re-check each file page just before upload.
3. **Avocado (4) is low resolution** (960 px wide): the page image would be
   960×540, not 1280×720 — never upscaled. Its file page gives no subject
   description beyond the title; grafting must be visible in the photo.
4. **Portrait crops** (1, 4) keep only ~40–45 % of the frame.
5. **Indicate changes.** CC BY 2.0 and CC BY-SA 2.0/4.0 require showing that
   the image was modified, and CC BY-SA copies stay CC BY-SA. Proposal:
   credit field "Creator / Wikimedia Commons (cropped, resized)", e.g.
   `Yganyana / Wikimedia Commons (cropped, resized)`; the guide page caption
   already links the credit to the file page and the licence to its deed.
